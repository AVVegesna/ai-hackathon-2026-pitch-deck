Section 1 30 seconds

Problem Statement

New Zealand has the largest commercial vessel camera network in the world, yet monitoring outcomes are limited by review capacity. The bottleneck is analysis, not hardware. As long as most footage is not reviewed, most ecological interactions remain practically invisible.

219 Vessels connected to the current camera network.
27% Recorded footage currently reviewed.
73% Unreviewed footage where bycatch events can go unseen.
Section 2 2 minutes 30 seconds

Our Solution

We introduce an AI review pipeline that turns passive recordings into always-on ecological intelligence. Instead of reviewing random slices, the system processes full footage coverage and escalates high-risk events for targeted human verification.

Ingest

Securely collect vessel footage and metadata streams at scale.

Detect

Use computer vision to flag bycatch, protected species, and anomaly patterns.

Prioritize

Rank events by ecological risk and confidence for expert review.

Report

Generate auditable dashboards for regulators, researchers, and operators.

Section 4 1 minute

Where The Future Will Be

The future is a national monitoring model where ecological accountability is continuous, measurable, and trusted. Full-footage AI review can evolve from enforcement support into predictive conservation, helping agencies and industry intervene earlier, reduce bycatch systematically, and prove progress with transparent evidence.

Vision: move from invisible marine impact to real-time ecological stewardship backed by complete data.
