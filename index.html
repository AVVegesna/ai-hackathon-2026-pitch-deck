Making the Invisible Visible: Ending the 73% Blind Spot in New Zealand Fisheries.

73% of footage remains unseen SDG 14

Problem Statement

The cameras are already there. The real bottleneck is converting footage into protection decisions fast enough to matter.

The Gap

Only a fraction of vessel footage is reviewed, creating a persistent blind spot in bycatch monitoring.

Why It Matters

Unseen events mean vulnerable species can be harmed without timely intervention or reliable evidence.

Current State

Manual review and self-reporting are too slow and too limited to deliver full, trusted transparency.

219 vessels already equipped with on-board cameras.
37,000 bycatch-related events captured every year.
73% of footage remains unreviewed, where impacts can stay invisible.

Our Solution

We introduce an AI review pipeline that turns passive recordings into always-on ecological intelligence. Instead of reviewing random slices, the system processes full footage coverage and escalates high-risk events for targeted human verification.

Ingest

Securely collect vessel footage and metadata streams at scale.

Detect

Use computer vision to flag bycatch, protected species, and anomaly patterns.

Prioritize

Rank events by ecological risk and confidence for expert review.

Report

Generate auditable dashboards for regulators, researchers, and operators.

How We Differentiate

We are not adding another camera layer. We are building accountability infrastructure that closes the gap between data capture and ecological action.

Coverage First

Designed for 100% footage analysis, not sample-based review.

Human + AI Loop

AI does throughput; experts do judgement and policy interpretation.

Conservation Outcomes

Outputs are framed for species protection and transparent enforcement.
